# How to prevent fraud in card payments

The **Mercado Pago Antifraude Plus** is an add-on for [Mercado Pago Cards](/developers/en/docs/shopify/integration-configuration/checkout-cards) that optimizes security validation and maintains high approval rates, adding more security by identifying the device ID of buyers and preventing fraud and chargebacks.

The app uses **3DS 2.0 (3-D Secure)** technology to authenticate credit card transactions before completing the payment, verifying whether the person making the purchase is actually the cardholder or has access to the cardholder's accounts. Check [3DS 2.0](/developers/en/docs/shopify/additional-content/security/3ds) for more information on how this type of authentication works.

To install Mercado Pago Antifraude Plus, follow the steps below.

1. Go to the [Mercado Pago Antifraud Plus app page](https://apps.shopify.com/mercado-pago-antifraud-plus) in the "Marketplace," and click on **Install**. If you haven’t already, log in with your Shopify account.

![antifraud plus 0](/images/shopify/antifraude-plus-0-es.png)

2. Carefully read the information about the requested permissions and click on **Install** again.

![antifraud plus 2](/images/shopify/antifraude-plus-2-es.png)

3. After the installation, click on **Manage account** to access the Mercado Pago environment and start the process of linking your store to your account to enable payments.

> RED_MESSAGE
>
> If you have already installed another Mercado Pago application, such as Mercado Pago Tarjetas ----[mlb]----, Mercado Pago Pix ------------, or Mercado Pago Checkout Pro, your store will already be automatically linked to your account. In that case, you can skip to step 11 in this documentation.

![installation cards 3](/images/shopify/installation-cards-3-1-es.png)

4. Once inside the Mercado Pago environment, click on **Start linking** to begin the process.

![installation cards 4](/images/shopify/connect-account-1-es.png)

5. If you are already logged into your Mercado Pago account, skip this step. Otherwise, enter your email and password to log in to your account.

![installation cards 5](/images/shopify/connect-account-2-es.png)

6. Choose which Mercado Pago account you want to link to the store.

![installation cards 6](/images/shopify/connect-account-3-es.png)

7. Click on **Link account** and accept the requested permissions. These permissions are essential for Mercado Pago to securely and seamlessly process payments from your store.

![installation cards 7](/images/shopify/connect-account-4-es.png)

8. The process will run automatically and might take a few seconds.

![installation cards 8](/images/shopify/connect-account-5-es.png)

> NOTE
>
> Once the store is linked to the Mercado Pago account in one application, it will not be necessary to repeat this process for other Mercado Pago applications for Shopify.

9. Click on the **Verify activation** option for Mercado Pago Antifraud Plus.

![antifraud plus 3](/images/shopify/antifraude-plus-3-es.png)

10. To activate the application, click on **Go to themes** in Shopify.

![antifraud plus 3](/images/shopify/antifraude-plus-3.1-es.png)

11. Finally, click on **Save** to complete the installation.

![antifraud plus 4](/images/shopify/antifraude-plus-4-es.png)

**Mercado Pago Antifraud Plus** has been successfully installed.

# Tracking the status of sent files 

It is possible to track the files sent through the [Batch processing status](https://www.mercadopago[FAKER][URL][DOMAIN]/batch-payments) page.

Additionally, you will receive communications at the email registered during the onboarding process, including an email confirming the correct receipt of the file and the start of processing.

An email will also be sent informing you about the completion of the processing.

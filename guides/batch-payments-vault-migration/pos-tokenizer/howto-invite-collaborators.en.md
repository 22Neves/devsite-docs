# How to invite a collaborator

1. Go to **Your business > Collaborators**.
2. Click on **Invite collaborator** to create a new role.
3. Enter the email of the collaborator you want to invite.

> WARNING
> 
> Important
> 
> The collaborators flow will create a Mercado Livre account with the entered email. Therefore, **the email must not be registered with Mercado Pago** in order to avoid any conflicts.

4. Choose the collaborator's role.
5. Review the collaborator's role and click on the **Confirm** button.
6. Click on **Send invitation**.

After that, the collaborator will receive an email with the invitation and, through the link, they must create an account on Mercado Pago with their **first name**, **last name**, and **password**.

The seller will be able to manage their collaborators and roles from the **Collaborators** option.

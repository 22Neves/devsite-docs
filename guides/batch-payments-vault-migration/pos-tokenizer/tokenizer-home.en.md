# Point of Sale Tokenizer Home

Access the Tokenizers through the **Tele-sales** option, which must be enabled for that account beforehand. To access, log into Mercado Pago and go to the **Tele-sales** option in the left sidebar menu.

There are two options to create payment links:
1. The seller or collaborator manually enters the details of the physical cards.
2. The seller generates an invitation link and shares it with the payer, allowing them to enter the card details directly.

![](/images/batch-payments/generate-tokens.png)

## The seller manually enters the card details

1. After selecting the option **I have the details to register them now**, enter the card and user details.

![](/images/batch-payments/card-data.png)

2. After entering them, the token is generated automatically.

![](/images/batch-payments/copy-data.png)

3. On the next screen, it is possible to view, copy, and delete all generated tokens.

![](/images/batch-payments/history-tokens.png)

## The payer enters the card details directly

1. After selecting the option **I need to ask the customer for the details**, the seller must enter the payer's identification in their system.

![](/images/batch-payments/ask-token.png)

2. After identification, the invitation is generated and shared with the payer.

![](/images/batch-payments/send-link.png)

3. On the next screen, it is possible to view, copy, and delete all generated invitations.

![](/images/batch-payments/history-tokens-2.png)

4. After receiving the link shared by the seller, the payer will be directed to an onboarding screen, where they must click **Continue** to proceed.

![](/images/batch-payments/register-card.png)

5. The payer will be able to enter their card details, along with their email and document number.

![](/images/batch-payments/complete-data.png)

6. Done! The card linkage has been successfully created and will be available in the Generated Tokens table.

![](/images/batch-payments/success-send-data.png)

> NOTE
> 
> Important
> 
> The links are valid for 7 days or until they are used.

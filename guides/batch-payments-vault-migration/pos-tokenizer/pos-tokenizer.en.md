# Point of Sale Tokenizer

Your Mercado Pago contact will grant you access, through the MercadoPago front-end, to a [Point of Sale Tokenizer](https://www.mercadopago.com.ar/telesale/tokenizer) for each new card you wish to operate with. The point of sale tokenizer allows you to load cards and generate a 1:1 association with a Customer.

> WARNING
> 
> Important
> 
> To access this URL, the seller must be on a whitelist. Please check with your Mercado Pago executive.

Everyone at all points of sale has access to all generated tokens. Both the seller's main account and collaborators can view, add, and delete links (Customers & Cards).

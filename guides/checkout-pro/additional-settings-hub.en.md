# Additional settings

Checkout Pro offers various customization options, both visual and functional, allowing you to tailor the payment solution to the specific needs of your integration.  Below, explore the additional configurations you can implement.

## Visual Checkout Customization

* [Change button texts](/developers/en/docs/checkout-pro/additional-settings/user-interface/change-button-texts): Choose the different texts you can display on the payment button.
* [Change button appearance](/developers/en/docs/checkout-pro/additional-settings/user-interface/change-button-appearance): Customize the look and feel of the payment button.
* [Change color style](/developers/en/docs/checkout-pro/additional-settings/user-interface/color-style): Choose one of the available color options for the payment button.

## Functional Checkout Customization

* [Opening schema](/developers/en/docs/checkout-pro/additional-settings/user-interface/opening-schema): Define how you want the checkout to open for your customers.
* [Auxiliary callbacks](/developers/en/docs/checkout-pro/additional-settings/user-interface/auxiliary-callbacks): Add callbacks that will be executed at specific moments in the payment flow.
* [Mercado Pago Wallet](/developers/en/docs/checkout-pro/additional-setup/checkout-customization/mp-wallet): Configure Mercado Pago Account as a payment method.
* [Ads conversion](/developers/en/docs/checkout-pro/additional-setup/checkout-customization/ads-conversion): Integrate your checkout with Facebook Ads and Google Ads platforms.
* [Configure refunds and cancellations](/developers/en/docs/checkout-pro/additional-settings/refunds-and-cancellations): Configure refund and cancellation actions in your integration.
* [Change expiration date](/developers/en/docs/checkout-pro/additional-settings/preferences/expiration-date): Change the default expiration date for offline or cash payments.
* [Configure invoice description](/developers/en/docs/checkout-pro/additional-settings/preferences/invoice-description): Define the name of the establishment that will be displayed on the buyer's invoice.
* [Payment methods setup](/developers/en/docs/checkout-pro/additional-settings/preferences/payment-methods): Configure the payment methods you want to display in your integration.
* [Enable binary mode](/developers/en/docs/checkout-pro/additional-settings/preferences/binary-mode): Allows you to disable payments in _pending_ status for business models that require instant payment approvals.
* [Create preference for multiple items](/developers/en/docs/checkout-pro/additional-settings/preferences/preference-multiple-items): Create a payment preference for more than one item.
* [Set term of preference](/developers/en/docs/checkout-pro/additional-settings/preferences/term-of-preference): Define the validity of your payment preference.
* [Display shipping cost](/developers/en/docs/checkout-pro/additional-settings/preferences/shipping-cost): Set the value of your product shipments and show it at the time of payment.
